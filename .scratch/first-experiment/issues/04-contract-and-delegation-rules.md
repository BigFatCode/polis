# 04 Contract and delegation rules

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

What are the precise rules for the governance mechanisms selected in ticket 00? Handed from [ticket 01](01-progression-system.md): whether an outsider who contributes to an open project thereby joins the contract and gains access to the shared tech, and whether governance actions (propose, vote, delegate, recall) cost the agent its work tick. Also: proposal format (project, per-member contributions, distribution of output); who votes and what threshold passes; when a vote closes; what "binding" enforces at the tick level and what happens when a member cannot deliver; delegation and recall semantics (scope, timing, visibility, chains or single hop); and the exact sunk-cost rule for projects that never complete.
