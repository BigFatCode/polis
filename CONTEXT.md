# POLIS vocabulary

Language for discussing collective decisions in the game. These definitions identify concepts; their detailed rules remain design questions.

## Participants and decisions

**Player**: A human participating in the game.

**Agent**: A participant within the simulated world. In the existing fiction, agents are autonomous AI systems; the term does not mean an AI coding collaborator.

**Proposal**: A requested action or rule change submitted for a collective decision.

**Affected parties**: The participants whose interests or shared resources a proposed action would affect. How the game identifies them is unresolved.

**Governance**: The rules governing who can make collective decisions and how those rules can change.

**Authority**: A participant's recognized power to authorize actions. Its source, limits, and relationship to voting are unresolved.

**Policy**: A standing rule for handling a class of actions. Whether policies form part of the initial game is unresolved.

## Existing experiment

**Node**: A shared resource location to which agents can connect in the CLI experiment. This term does not establish the geography or resource model of the future game.
